SayItToMe Child Safety Standards

Effective date: May 2026

SayItToMe.me (com.sayittome.app), developed by PatagonicosADMIN, strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, sexualization of minors, solicitation involving minors, and any harmful behavior involving children.

Zero Tolerance Policy

SayItToMe has a zero-tolerance policy against CSAE and CSAM. Any content, profile, message, media, or behavior involving child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or sexualized minors is strictly forbidden.

Reporting and User Feedback

Users can report content, profiles, conversations, and media through the app. Reports are reviewed and may result in content removal, account suspension, permanent bans, or reports to the appropriate authorities.

Moderation and Enforcement

SayItToMe may remove content, suspend accounts, ban users, and report illegal activity to law enforcement or child safety organizations where required.
